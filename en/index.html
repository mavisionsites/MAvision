Process

Our process

Five steps to turn a need into a result.

  1. Analyze

    We assess the scenario, the current site and the market.

    We assess the structure, design, performance and content of the existing site.

  2. Understand

    We identify goals, audience and points to improve.

    Gradual changes, validated with you, until we reach the expected result.

  3. Structure

    We organize content, pages and the customer journey.

    We configure titles and content to rank better on Google.

  4. Design

    We define the design and experience on every device.

    We polish the look and the experience on every screen size.

  5. Develop

    We build, test and publish the project.

    We publish the site and hand over the project ready to use.

About

About MAvision

MAvision was born from a recurring problem in the market: many companies invested in websites that delivered no results because they lacked proper structure, planning and technical care.

So we took a different approach. Before building, we analyze the existing website, understand how it was conceived and structured, and only then define the improvements needed.

With this method we refine the design, optimize access across devices, rank the site on Google with SEO-optimized content and centralize customer contact in a single service channel.
